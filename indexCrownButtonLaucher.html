Privacy Policy

Application: Crown Button · Last updated August 25, 2026

This policy describes how consultantbpm@gmail.com collects, uses, and protects your information when you use Crown Button, our Wear OS companion application.

1

Information Collection and Use

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information.

We do not use advertising networks, we do not build user profiles, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

2

Device Identifier and Purchase Status

To keep the one-day trial from being reset by reinstalling, and to restore a purchase you have already made, the application sends a small record to a private endpoint operated by the developer: a device identifier (the Android ID assigned by your device), the date the trial started, and whether a purchase exists.

This record contains no name, no email address, and no contact details. It is used only to determine whether the premium features are unlocked. It is never used for advertising or analytics, and it is never shared with third parties. If the endpoint cannot be reached, the application simply continues using the record stored on your device.

3

Installed Applications and Usage Statistics

Crown Button is a launcher-style application: to let you assign a hardware button to an application, it reads the list of applications installed on your watch. This list is exchanged only between your own phone and your own watch, over the Google Wearable Data Layer, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The "Recents" category is built from Android usage statistics. That permission is optional and is granted by you, explicitly, from your device settings; if you do not grant it, the category stays empty. The statistics are read on the device and are never transmitted.

4

Accessibility Service

On the watch, Crown Button uses an accessibility service for a single purpose: detecting hardware button presses and drawing the on-screen selection frame so you can navigate without touching the display.

The service does not read, record, store, or transmit the contents of your screen, your keystrokes, your messages, or anything you type. No screen content ever leaves your watch.

5

Purchases

The premium upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Your payment details are handled by Google and are never seen, received, or stored by us. We receive only the confirmation that a purchase exists.

6

Log Data

In the event of a crash, Google Play may collect diagnostic information such as device model, operating system version, and the technical details of the error. This is provided by Google Play itself and is available to us in aggregated form through the Play Console.

The application contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising libraries of its own.

7

Your Settings and How to Delete Your Data

Your button mappings, favourites, and thresholds are stored on your phone and your watch. Uninstalling the application removes them from that device.

To have the entitlement record described in section 2 deleted, write to us at consultantbpm@gmail.com and we will remove it. Please note that deleting it also removes the record of any purchase or trial associated with that device.

8

Children

Crown Button is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9

Security

We make efforts to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your data, including transmitting the record described in section 2 over an encrypted HTTPS connection. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10

Links to Other Sites

The application can open Google Play pages — for the premium upgrade, for an application you selected, or for our other applications. Those pages are governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by this one.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last changed.

12

Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about our privacy policy, or you need support with the application, please contact us at consultantbpm@gmail.com.
