Privacy Policy

Application: Crown Button Launcher · Last updated August 29, 2026

Crown Button Launcher does not collect any data. Nothing about you or your device is sent to us. This policy explains what the application reads on your device, why, and what never leaves it. Questions: consultantbpm@gmail.com.

1

No Accounts, No Personal Information

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information.

We do not use advertising networks, we do not build user profiles, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

2

No Device Identifier Is Collected

The application does not send a device identifier, or any other record about you, to us or to anyone else. There is no developer-operated server behind this application.

An earlier version of Crown Button Launcher (before August 29, 2026) sent a small entitlement record — a device identifier, the trial start date, and whether a purchase existed — to a private endpoint, so that a purchase could be restored and a trial could not be reset by reinstalling. That mechanism has been removed. Your premium status now comes exclusively from Google Play, tied to your Google account rather than to your device.

3

Installed Applications and Usage Statistics

Crown Button Launcher is a launcher-style application: to let you assign a hardware button to an application, it reads the list of applications installed on your watch. This list is exchanged only between your own phone and your own watch, over the Google Wearable Data Layer, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The "Recents" category is built from Android usage statistics. That permission is optional and is granted by you, explicitly, from your device settings; if you do not grant it, the category stays empty. The statistics are read on the device and are never transmitted.

4

Accessibility Service

On the watch, Crown Button Launcher uses an accessibility service for a single purpose: detecting hardware button presses and drawing the on-screen selection frame so you can navigate without touching the display.

The service does not read, record, store, or transmit the contents of your screen, your keystrokes, your messages, or anything you type. No screen content ever leaves your watch.

5

Purchases

The premium upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Your payment details are handled by Google and are never seen, received, or stored by us. The application only asks Google Play whether a purchase exists for the account signed in on the device.

6

The Only Network Connections

The application makes two kinds of network requests, and neither one sends anything about you:

— Google Play Billing, to check whether the premium upgrade has been purchased.

— A public list of our other applications, downloaded from a Google Sheets address so the "Apps" screen can show what else we make. This is a plain download; no information about you is included in the request.

7

Log Data

In the event of a crash, Google Play may collect diagnostic information such as device model, operating system version, and the technical details of the error. This is provided by Google Play itself and is available to us in aggregated form through the Play Console.

The application contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising libraries of its own.

8

Your Settings and How to Delete Your Data

Your button mappings, favourites, shortcuts, and press thresholds are stored on your phone and on your watch, and nowhere else. Uninstalling the application removes them from that device.

Because we hold no record about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and no deletion request is necessary. Your purchase is held by Google Play, under your Google account; that is managed through Google, not through us.

9

Children

Crown Button Launcher is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10

Security

The strongest protection this application offers is that your data stays where it is: your settings never leave your devices, so there is no server of ours that could be breached, and nothing of yours for us to lose.

The two network requests described in section 6 use encrypted HTTPS connections. As with any software, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

11

Links to Other Sites

The application can open Google Play pages — for the premium upgrade, for an application you selected, or for our other applications. Those pages are governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by this one.

12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last changed.

13

Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about our privacy policy, or you need support with the application, please contact us at consultantbpm@gmail.com.
